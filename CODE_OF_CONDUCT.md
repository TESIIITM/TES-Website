# Code of Conduct

TES works on three ideas: no bias, transparency, and open feedback. We expect everyone in this project (issues, PRs, reviews, and anywhere else we talk) to stick to them.

This is based on the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## The short version

Be respectful. Judge work by its quality, not by who wrote it or what year they're in. Give feedback on the code, not the person, and take feedback the same way.

Harassment isn't tolerated here, whatever someone's gender, identity, background, religion, caste, ability, or experience level.

## Not okay

- Insults, trolling, or personal attacks
- Sexual language, imagery, or attention
- Harassment, in public or in private
- Sharing someone's private details without their permission
- Copying someone else's work and passing it off as yours
- Spam PRs opened just to get a contribution counted

## Reporting

If something happens, email [tes@iiitm.ac.in](mailto:tes@iiitm.ac.in). Please don't post it in a public issue. Reports are kept private, and a maintainer who's involved won't handle the report.

## What maintainers may do

Depending on how serious it is, maintainers may:

1. Send a private warning
2. Ask the person to stay away from the people involved for a while
3. Ban them temporarily
4. Ban them permanently, for repeated or serious behaviour

Maintainers can also edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and PRs that break these rules.
